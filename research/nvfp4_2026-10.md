# NVFP4 checkpoints, October 2026 — GLM-5.3 and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash

**Purpose:** after the September sweep (`research/nvfp4_2026-09.md`), two
models had no measured NVFP4 arm. GLM-5.3 used NVIDIA's GLM-5.2 recipe as a
projection, and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash had none. NVIDIA has since released
checkpoints for both. Read 2026-10-02; every model now has a measured arm.

## 1. Method

The method is the September sweep's: every safetensors shard header is read
with HTTP range requests, each tensor's bytes are summed from dtype × shape,
and tensors are binned by name. The base checkpoint is read the same way
and compared tensor by tensor. Revisions read:

| Repo | Revision | Created | Shards | Bytes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `nvidia/GLM-5.3-NVFP4` | `e3b8f9f2` | 2026-09-14 | 50 | 464,115,789,896 |
| `zai-org/GLM-5.3` (FP8 base) | `aca966e4` | 2026-08-25 | 141 | 755,617,140,416 |
| `nvidia/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-NVFP4` | `3431dde3` | 2026-09-16 | 48 | 527,273,322,840 |
| `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash` (native) | `2cba9e42` | 2026-09-10 | 48 | 510,286,023,000 |

The DeepSeek base total matches `w_resident` to the byte. Per-expert bytes
include each projection's two F32 global scales (input and weight), 24 B per
expert, which the GLM-5.2 projection had left out.

## 2. GLM-5.3

Bytes by tensor role, FP8 base vs NVFP4:

| Role | FP8 | NVFP4 | Delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| Routed experts, layers 3–77 | 724,952,678,400 | 407,686,809,600 | −317,265,868,800 |
| Attention (MLA) | 12,875,081,856 | 25,743,464,448 | +12,868,382,592 |
| Indexers | 200,991,168 | 393,619,968 | +192,628,800 |
| Shared experts | 2,831,846,400 | 5,662,310,400 | +2,830,464,000 |
| Dense MLP, layers 0–2 | 679,643,136 | 679,477,320 | −165,816 |
| Gates, norms, embed, lm_head | 4,044,266,496 | 4,044,266,496 | 0 |
| MTP layer 78 | 10,032,632,960 | 19,905,841,664 | +9,873,208,704 |

The recipe quantizes only the routed experts of the 75 main MoE layers:
21,233,688 B per expert (U8-packed FP4, E4M3 block-16 scales). Attention,
indexers, shared experts and the MTP layer, including its experts, are BF16.
Unlike NVIDIA's GLM-5.2 recipe, the three dense MLP layers stay FP8.

The always-active read follows the September convention: the FP8 constant
(18.92e9) plus the measured delta on the same tensors (attention, indexers,
shared experts, dense MLP: +15,891,309,576). The result is 34,811,309,576 B,
0.49e9 below the projection because the dense MLPs were not upcast.

```
nvfp4_w = [464115789896, 34811309576,
           8 × 21,233,688 × 75   = 12740212800,
           256 × 21,233,688 × 75 = 407686809600]
```

The projection was `[464.8e9, 35.30e9, 12740198400, 407686348800]`, so
resident bytes move −0.15% and the always-active read −1.4%.

## 3. DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash

Only the routed experts of the 40 main layers differ (15,360 experts). The
native checkpoint packs them as I8-stored FP4 with E8M0 block-32 scales
(18,800,640 B per expert). NVIDIA repacks them as U8-stored FP4 with E4M3
block-16 scales plus two F32 globals per projection (19,906,584 B per
expert, +5.9%). Every other tensor is byte-identical, MTP included.
The checkpoint delta (+16,987,299,840 B) equals 15,360 × the per-expert
delta exactly.

```
nvfp4_w = [527273322840, 8522921408,
           6 × 19,906,584 × 40   = 4777580160,
           384 × 19,906,584 × 40 = 305765130240]
```

This is the 0731 result again: the NVFP4 arm is 3.3% heavier than native and
exists for the NVFP4 kernel path, not for bytes. The explorer prices what
the checkpoint weighs. On 4×B300 at TP4 the KV pool falls from 592.2M to
573.1M tokens; 1×B300 still does not fit.

## 4. Not priced

Community repacks of both models (`RadixArk/`, `Inferact/`, `modal-labs/`,
`AxionML/`, and others) exist. The NVIDIA checkpoints meet the study's bar,
so the community repacks were not read.
